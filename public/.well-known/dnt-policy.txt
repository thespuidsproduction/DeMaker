Do Not Track Compliance Policy — princey.dev
Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-09-01

princey.dev honours the Do Not Track header. It does so trivially, because
there is nothing here to opt out of.

1. NO TRACKING OF ANY KIND
   This site sets no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no fingerprinting
   scripts, and no third-party tags. It does not build a profile of you, and
   it has nothing to share with anyone because it collects nothing to share.

2. COOKIES
   This site sets no cookies.

3. LOCAL STORAGE
   One key, `atelier-theme`, records your colour-scheme preference. It never
   leaves your browser, is never transmitted anywhere, and is readable only
   by this origin. Clearing site data removes it and nothing else is lost.

4. LOGS
   The hosting provider may retain standard request logs (IP address, user
   agent, path, timestamp) for operational and abuse-prevention purposes. I
   do not query these logs to identify individuals, do not join them to any
   other dataset, and do not retain them beyond the provider's own default
   window.

5. THIRD PARTIES
   Typefaces are served self-hosted from this origin. No request made while
   loading this site goes to an analytics, advertising or social platform.

6. WHEN A DNT SIGNAL IS RECEIVED
   No change in behaviour is required, because the behaviour it asks for is
   already the only behaviour this site has.

7. CHANGES
   Any change to this policy will be published here with a new version
   number and effective date.
